SIKH TIMELINE

Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026.

Information in the app

Sikh Timeline does not require an account. The app does not collect personal information or send your searches or language preference to the developer. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

The timeline content and fonts are bundled with the app. Your language preference is stored locally on your device. You can remove that preference by deleting the app and its data.

External links and services

Some timeline entries link to Wikipedia in your external browser. If you open a link, that website's privacy policy applies. Your browser and the website may process information about your visit.

Apple may separately process App Store or diagnostic information under Apple's policies and your device settings. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose service may process technical information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

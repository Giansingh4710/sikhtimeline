Sikh Timeline

Explore the Sikh Gurus, their families, notable Sikhs, and historical events through an interactive timeline in English and Punjabi.

Using the app

Drag to move through the years, pinch to zoom, and tap a person or event for details. Use the category buttons to filter the timeline and the search button to find entries in either language.

The timeline works offline. Reference links open in your browser and require an internet connection.
